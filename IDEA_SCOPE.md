# IDEA LOCK · Build Sprint

## The idea, in one line

FinSightX reads bank alert emails from Gmail and answers “Can I afford this ₹40k phone this week?” from your real income, spending and upcoming bills.

**Why me:** I have worked in product strategy and analytics for a fintech for the last four years. I am passionate about personal finance.

## GOAL

**Goal is Money:** Save money by tracking it efficiently and make financial decisions smartly and quickly.

**Delta 4 — the steps today → the steps with my product:**

- **Today:** Track income and spending mentally or on Excel sheets. The process is inefficient, information is not readily available, and personal finances feel out of control.
- **With my product (V1):** Efficient, automatic tracking of where money flows, plus a calculation of whether a purchase fits within the user's safe-to-spend amount.
- **With my product (V2):** Make financial decisions with confidence through AI and human, unbiased, personalised advisory.

**The sin it rides (optional):** Greed, possibly Sloth.

## USER

**The trigger — when the pain hits:**

1. The user realises they are out of money and still have days to go before their salary arrives.
2. The user is considering a big purchase and wonders, “Can I afford it now?”

**Today's path, step by step:**

1. Realise they are out of money when they need something but cannot buy it.
2. Go back to calculating on paper or mentally when they received their last income and where they spent it.
3. Realise they could have avoided or delayed a few purchases if they had known about upcoming expenses.
4. Decide to maintain a ledger in the future, but do not follow through and end up in the same situation.

**Who they trust on this decision:** Themselves, their emails and SMS messages to remember expenses.

**Would they pay?** Money-tracking solutions already exist in the market. Some users may pay for this.

## PRODUCT

**Onboarding — how a first-time user feels the value fastest:** Automatically pull financial transaction information from Gmail and show it in a clear visual.

**The core loop:** The user comes to see their financial position for the month and their safe-to-spend number.

**Coming back (optional):** To be defined.

### V1 limits — next two weeks

- Money tracking and affordability calculations.
- Answer “Can I afford this?” by comparing the purchase amount with the user's safe-to-spend amount, calculated from real income, spending and upcoming bills.
- Read bank alert emails from Gmail on the web.
- No advisory.
- No account aggregator.
- No SMS.

**The AI-first part:** AI is at the core of the intended experience: answering “Can I afford this ₹40k phone this week?” using real income, spending and upcoming bills.

**Scope decision:** “Can I afford this?” is a V1 calculation. Personalised AI or human advisory belongs in V2.

## MARKET

**Tailwinds — builder observations, not yet verified:** Huge growth in searches for “how to find a good personal advisor in India.” Competitors are around three to four years old, with some funded by prominent Indian VCs. AI could provide an edge.

**Competitors:** Fold looks like a good competitor. My understanding is that they are not in the advisory space. We want to build an audience first, then move into advisory through AI or humans. Screenshots and specific flows to be added.

**Size and fit:** Around 50–100 people in my extended network fit the target user.
