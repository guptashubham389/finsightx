## 1. The job
When I go to the mall for shopping on a weekend, I want to buy an item and when i see the price, i want to know whether i can afford this now, so I can buy the stuff guilt free without running out of my money before my next paycheck comes. Today I have to do mental math of my upcoming expenses and if I can afford it or not
Other moments it happens: I go to a super market to buy the stuff i need for eg. food etc. and i realise that i don’t have money in my account. I feel guilty about the extra money I spent last weekend on an expensive dinner. And I want to be better in planning my expenses.

Who, by situation - Urban salaried individual who receives their salary mostly at the start of the month
Today they hire: [what they use or do now, including nothing] - do mental math inefficiently, use excel sheet to create budget
How they want to feel: They want to feel that they have their money in control, 
How they want to look to others: confident before buying anything with anyone


## 2. The switch
What they'd fire: manual way of tracking their expenses

The forces that matter 
Push, outside them -  not able to keep track of their expense and estimate  their upcoming expenses and force to take unnecessary credit
Pull, inside them : 
Anxiety - manual tracking
Habit (the way they already do it) - mental math

What the product does about each: [push] -> captures the expenses by email statements and manual entries, reconcile it where it’s doubtful 

The one worry onboarding must remove: [one line, in their words] - Stay in control of your expenses and avoid the credit trap

## 3. The core flow
Today: want to buy an expensive item -> thinks if i can afford it to buy it now or not -> do mental math, when i would receive my next paycheck, is there any upcoming expense -> get rough idea -> take decision to buy/not buy with low confidence
With finsightx -> want to buy an expensive item -> check their safe to spend number on the app -> double sure by looking at upcoming expenses -> adds if anything is missed -> be confident to know that they can purchase it now

Things it takes to get the job done today: nothing, mental math · With my product: just open the app and know the ‘safe to spend’ number

What must not happen: redundant entries; double counting

## 4. Onboarding
First value (the moment it first does the job for them): know your safe to spend number? We analyse your spend pattern and give you the number that you can spend guilt free.
The smallest commitment we ask for: users just have to link their email and give permission to read the bank statements - 
email linking and permission to read bank alerts
The worry it removes (from section 2): manual tracking of expenses


Login: yes, ask them to connect to gmail and give permission to read alerts
What we don't ask on day one: we don't ask them any personal information or any other financial information. 
What we ask later, and when: way to enter their cash transactions and income details that may not be captured in the emails


## 5. v1
Does: able to connect to email/gmail and fetch the UPI alerts and bank statements of any bank. Calculate the inflow and outflow, build the personal ledger and predict the recurring spend and income. Calculate monthly safe to spend number
Doesn't (not this sprint: parked, not forgotten): connect using account aggregator and get the full view of financials of the person/household
Nice to have enter cash spend details, add the details by voice
How I'll know it worked: able to do the job with good accuracy ( ₹1,000.), user feedback is good 

## 6. The riskiest guess
If this is false, the product is pointless: categorise credit and debits to forecast the upcoming expenses correctly and should not be off by 1000 rupees


## 7. Milestones
1. Export the last three months of bank alerts manually, Check the current date, test the past months to forecast the expense for remaining days in the month, should be within ₹1,000 in a month. Based on that, calculate the ‘safe to spend today’ number.
2. connect the email to the app, app reads the bank alerts and create a personal ledger to categorise debit and credit
3. Must give an option to enter the upcoming expenses or income in the app

ending with: I can close it, reopen it, and my data is still there.
